How XP works

Gym day+20
Skill day (purple: skills only). Counts for the weekly quest and the streak+10
New level on your skill ladder (e.g. tuck front lever), each level once+30
Win a duel (challenge a friend from their profile)+50
New all-time record+50
Streak: weeks in a row with 3+ gym days. From the 2nd week, each week pays +10 per week of streakup to +50
Beat your last time

No XP just for logging sets: 2 hard sets earn the same as 5. Extra rep = one more rep per set (on average) than last time at the same weight. +2.5 kg = more weight than last time. Max = the most one log can earn: beat last time by one step and set a record. Holds count seconds: every 5 extra seconds = one extra rep.

Difficulty: Easy ×1, Medium ×1.25, Hard ×1.5, picked when you add an exercise. Harder lifts pay more for the same progress.
